Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us improve your experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you interact with our site.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for our website to function properly (e.g., Calendly booking widget)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it
  • Performance Cookies: Help us monitor website performance and speed

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics: Used for website analytics. Learn more
  • Calendly: Used for booking consultations. Learn more

How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Check your browser's help section for instructions.
  • Opt-Out Tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.
  • Cookie Consent: Our website may display a cookie consent banner when you first visit.

What Happens If You Disable Cookies?

If you disable cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly. For example, the Calendly booking widget may not work, and we won't be able to analyse website traffic to improve our services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
