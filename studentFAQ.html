Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions students ask me.

How much do lessons cost? +

Lessons start from 100zł per hour. The exact price depends on the type of lesson, your level, and the package you choose. I offer single lessons as well as discounted packages for students who book multiple sessions.

Book a free consultation and I'll give you a personalised quote based on your needs.

How do I book a lesson? +

You can book a free 20-minute consultation through the Calendly widget on this website. During the consultation, we'll discuss your goals, assess your level, and find a time that works for you.

My schedule is very busy, so I recommend booking as early as possible to secure your preferred time slot.

What ages and levels do you teach? +

All ages and all levels. I've taught students from retirement age (70+) down to 6-year-old children. Whether you're a complete beginner or an advanced learner, I can help.

I specialise in:

  • Exam preparation (IELTS, Cambridge, Matura, 8th grade)
  • Business and Medical English
  • General English – from conversational to academic
  • English competitions
  • Any other English need – just ask!
Where are lessons held? +

All lessons are delivered online via video call. I'm flexible with platforms – we can use Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Skype, Google Meet, or any other video calling service you're comfortable with.

This means you can learn from anywhere, whether you're at home, at work, or even on holiday.

What's your cancellation policy? +

You can reschedule or cancel your lesson up to 2 hours before the scheduled start time at no charge.

If you cancel within 2 hours of the lesson, the full lesson fee may be charged. I understand emergencies happen, so just let me know as soon as possible.

What are your available hours? +

I teach Monday to Saturday. I do not teach on Sundays.

My schedule is very busy, so spaces fill up quickly. Book your free consultation to secure your spot as soon as possible.

Where are you based and what's your background? +

I'm based in the United Kingdom, where I've lived for nearly a decade.

I hold a Biomedical Science degree from the University of Coventry in the UK. As a young scientist, I specialised in language development, which gives me a unique understanding of how people learn and process language.

I've been teaching English for over 10 years and have delivered more than 10,000 lessons.

Do you offer a free trial lesson? +

Yes! I offer a free 20-minute consultation where we can discuss your goals, assess your level, and see if we're a good fit for each other.

If you book a full lesson and you're not happy, I guarantee your first lesson free – you're guaranteed to be happy or you don't pay.

How quickly will I see improvement? +

Most students see noticeable improvement within 4-6 weeks of regular lessons. Of course, this depends on your starting level, how often you practise, and how much time you dedicate to studying between lessons.

90% of my students improve by at least one grade, and 98% say their lessons made a significant difference.

Do you prepare students for specific exams? +

Absolutely! I specialise in exam preparation and have a 100% pass rate for IELTS and Cambridge exams.

I prepare students for:

  • IELTS (Academic and General)
  • Cambridge C1 Advanced (CAE)
  • Cambridge C2 Proficiency (CPE)
  • Polish Matura (all levels)
  • 8th grade exam
  • English competitions (regional and national)
