Business English: Key Phrases for Meetings & Presentations

📅 17 June 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ By Karolina

Business English can feel intimidating. Meetings, presentations, emails – there's a lot of professional language to learn. But the good news is that once you learn the key phrases, you'll feel much more confident.

Here are the essential phrases you need for meetings and presentations.

💼 Meetings

Opening a Meeting

"Thanks for joining everyone. Let's get started."

"Shall we begin?"

"The main purpose of this meeting is to..."

Making a Point

"From my perspective..."

"I'd like to highlight..."

"One important point is that..."

"I agree with that, however..."

Asking Questions

"Could you elaborate on that?"

"What are your thoughts on this?"

"Does anyone have any questions?"

Closing a Meeting

"To summarise the key points..."

"The next steps are..."

"I'll follow up with an email summarising the action items."

💬 Tip: In international meetings, people expect directness. Don't worry about being too direct – it's often seen as being efficient and professional.

📊 Presentations

Introducing Your Topic

"I'm going to talk about..."

"Today I'd like to present..."

"The aim of this presentation is to..."

Structuring Your Presentation

"First, I'll cover..."

"Next, I'll move on to..."

"Finally, I'll conclude with..."

Moving Between Slides

"Let's move on to the next point."

"As you can see on this slide..."

"This illustrates my point perfectly."

Summarising and Concluding

"To sum up..."

"In conclusion..."

"I hope this has given you a clear understanding of..."

"I'm happy to take any questions."

📧 Business Emails

Opening Phrases

"I hope you're well."

"Thank you for your email regarding..."

"I'm writing to enquire about..."

Making a Request

"I would appreciate it if you could..."

"Could you please send me..."

"I look forward to your response."

Closing Phrases

"I look forward to hearing from you."

"Please don't hesitate to contact me."

"Yours sincerely" (if you know the name)

"Yours faithfully" (if you don't know the name)

🎯 Key Tip: In British business culture, politeness is valued but so is clarity. Be polite, but also be clear about what you need and when you need it.

📈 Quick Reference: Common Business Phrases

  • "Touch base" – Have a quick conversation about something
  • "Get up to speed" – Become familiar with something
  • "Move the needle" – Make a significant impact
  • "Think outside the box" – Be creative
  • "On the same page" – Agree with someone
